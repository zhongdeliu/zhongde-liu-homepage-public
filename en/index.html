02 · Level system · scale 1:100

Architect

Then see how it fits together.

Across many projects and industries: frontend landscapes made of several apps, teams and interfaces - sometimes a modular monolith, sometimes micro-frontends. The pattern follows the problem, not the trend. What matters to me: clear contracts between UI and backend, deliberately scoped UI state, integration tests across boundaries and mock environments where teams build independently. To me, architecture means deciding in ways that make other teams faster.

03 · Level organization · scale 1:10,000

Leader

Leading from the workbench.

Today I lead the WebFrontends unit at MaibornWolff: people with passion and knowledge for web frontends, working in ever-changing projects with changing teams. What matters to me is that everyone grows with every project and carries that experience forward - through exchange, a shared attitude towards the craft and room to grow. Teams follow the same rules as good software: clear interfaces, loose coupling, plenty of trust.

To me, leadership and engineering aren’t opposites.
